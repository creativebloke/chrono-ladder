The honest read

Two questions. Answer one, and the pick is obvious.

Do you need to close the rings?

If the Move / Exercise / Stand rings and native Fitness app are the point, there's no workaround — it has to be Apple. The SE 2 is the cheapest watch here that does exactly what your Ultra does for the rings. Everything else only feeds steps.

Stop at Apple Watch SE 2, ~£219.

Or just steps in Health — with character?

If you only need the step count to land in Apple Health and you'd rather have looks, battery or toughness, go where your wrist and taste lead. For a watch that doesn't read as a tracker, the hybrid; for digital toughness, the octagon.

Stop at Withings ScanWatch 2 (~£300) — or G-Shock GBD-300 (~£160).
